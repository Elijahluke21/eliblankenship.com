Bible apps & study tools

5 links
  • Through the Word throughtheword.org

    I use it daily Pairs every chapter of the Bible with a ten-minute audio guide from a Bible teacher. Free, no ads. It's been my daily devotion for years, mostly on the drive to and from work. Their Revelation study is excellent, and I'm now going through the whole Bible from Genesis.

  • YouVersion Bible App bible.com

    I use it My pick for reading with a community. Reading plans with friends, verse images you can make and share, and audio and study guides. Built by Life.Church, free with no ads.

  • Olive Tree olivetree.com

    I use it A clean Bible reading app that flows well. I like pulling up different translations or passages side by side.

  • Blue Letter Bible blueletterbible.org

    I use it I mostly use it for the interlinear and concordance. Great for word studies in the Hebrew and Greek, and free from a nonprofit.

  • Logos logos.com

    I use it The biggest Bible software platform, with original-language tools, commentaries, and a huge library. The free app goes a long way on its own.

Study Bibles

1 link
  • Illustrated Study Bible (NLT) tyndale.com

    My study Bible Hundreds of photos, illustrations, infographics, and full-color maps built right into the study notes. It won the 2016 Christian Book Award for Bible of the Year. It's the one I use, and I love it.
