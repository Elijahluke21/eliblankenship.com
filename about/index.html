04 · About

Hi, I'm Eli.

I'm a software engineer in the Birmingham, Alabama area. My day job is building real-time video and imaging software in modern C++ and Qt, running on embedded NVIDIA Jetson hardware.

I got here the long way. Before software, I worked as an engineering technician: running IT and network installs, flying drones and processing the point clouds, and doing environmental permitting and monitoring work. That mix taught me to care about how things work in the field, not just on a whiteboard.

Outside of work, I manage the Christian rock band Nine Lashes, help lead a college and career group at church, and spend a lot of time in a long, slow study of the Bible. The maps on this site come out of that study.
