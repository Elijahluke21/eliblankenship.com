    The splits

      Colors show the tradition of the majority church (solid), or the main Christian body where Christians were a minority (faded). Modern country borders are only a reference grid: where a tradition held part of a modern country, only that part is shaded, using rough boxes. Arian (Homoian) Christianity among the Goths and Vandals, and the Maronites before their union with Rome, are not shown separately. Today's shading reflects affiliation, which in much of northern and western Europe is now largely nominal.
