    Acts 1:8 zones

      The rings are distances from Jerusalem, a schematic reading of 1:8 rather than political borders. They light up with the book's three movements: chapters 1–7, 8–12 and 13–28.

      Milestones

        Layers

        Places light up at the chapter where Acts first brings the message there, whether by preaching, by believers already present, or by travelers carrying it home (hollow markers). Coordinates are approximate for sites like the Gaza road and Fair Havens. The Roman frontier is traced for about AD 60, under Nero; client kingdoms such as Nabataea and Armenia sit outside it. The map uses an azimuthal projection centered on Jerusalem, so every straight-line distance from the city is true to scale. Coastlines: Natural Earth. One Roman mile ≈ 1.48 km.
