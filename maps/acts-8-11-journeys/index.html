    About this map

    Dates are rough. They assume Saul's conversion about AD 34, with Galatians' "after three years" counted from it; scholars place the conversion anywhere from about AD 31 to 36. Peter's tour has no fixed date beyond following the peace of Acts 9:31.

    Dashed rings mark places whose exact site is unknown: the baptism on the Gaza road, the Damascus road vision, Saul's stay in Arabia, and his years in Syria and Cilicia. Saul's next step, being brought from Tarsus to Antioch (Acts 11:25–26), falls just outside these passages.
